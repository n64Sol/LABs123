Labyrinths — Game Summary
Labyrinths is a browser-based multiplayer dungeon-crawler with a player-owned economy, built on a React + Canvas frontend and a Node/Express API server with PostgreSQL.

Core Concept
Every player owns a labyrinth — a procedurally generated dungeon they customize — and runs through other players' labyrinths for loot and XP. Owners earn a 20% cut of the loot their visitors collect. It's designed around a real player economy with Solana wallet integration (currently mocked, with on-chain USDC wired in).

The World — Streaming Overworld
An infinitely zoomable, chunk-streamed 2D top-down world map where every player's labyrinth occupies a plot in one of six biomes: Verdant Grove, Sunlit Ruins, Tidecaller, Crystal Caverns, Emberforge, and Astral Spire.
Each biome has its own visual theme, ambient particles (pollen, embers, water droplets, stars), and distinct floor tiles.
Zoom out to see the full world as stylized symbolic regions; zoom in for the detailed LPC sprite view.
Real-time presence: you can see other players walking around the map as composited LPC sprites.
Labyrinth popups open in-world (no page navigation) showing difficulty, loot ceiling, entry fee, biome, and leaderboard — then you click Enter to start a run.
Search: type a labyrinth or player name to fly the camera to their plot.
The Dungeon Run
Runs are real-time, client-simulated combat on a canvas-rendered tilemap. The server is authoritative only for completion and rewards.
Rooms are assembled from a hand-crafted ASCII room library, seeded per labyrinth with a depth/role arc (starter → mid → elite → boss). Owners can unlock new room types (by role and size) with gold, shaping their dungeon's arc.
Combat: melee and ranged attacks, projectiles, enemy AI chasing the player. Hit feedback includes flinch, hurt flashes, and tip-over death beats. Enemy types: grunt, elite, ranged, boss — each spawning in cleared positions with a runtime un-stick system.
Combat modes: Auto (auto-targets nearest enemy, auto-fires) or Manual (mouse-aim, hold to attack) — per-device preference set before entering.
Abilities: Ability Stones drop in runs and equip into two ability slots, granting active combat moves (with cooldowns).
Loot: at the end of a run, you receive drops sampled across rarities (Common → Legendary). Stones are weighted at ~20% of drops. Boss rooms give a rarity boost. Owner gets 20% cut of the loot value.
Character & Gear
Full LPC sprite compositor: the player character is layered from equipped gear — helmet, armor, shoulders, gloves, cape, boots, neck, pants, shield, and weapon — composited in real-time using a shared composeLoadoutSprite function.
147 weapons from the Universal LPC library (blades, maces, polearms, bows, staves), each with an 832×1344 sprite sheet carrying synthesized walk (all 4 directions) and slash animations.
Weapon archetypes (sword, glaive, maul, bow, dagger, axe, staff) have distinct stat profiles: swords are balanced, glaives have reach+sweep, mauls have big attack+slow, bows have crit+range.
Gear stats: attack, defense, speed, critChance, range, attackSpeed — stats are deterministic per (slot, rarity) from the tuned catalog.
Codex: a full item gallery showing every weapon and armor piece, grouped by rarity, with discovery tracking (which items the player has ever owned).
Gear comparison: equipping shows upgrade deltas; Best-in-Slot is surfaced on the loadout.
Multiplayer Features
Feature	What it does
Co-op runs	Up to 4 players team up, run the same dungeon together; difficulty scales; rewards split fairly server-side.
PvP duels	Challenge any overworld player (press F). The server runs a deterministic auto-battle; both players watch a synchronized arena replay with HP bars and ability beats. Outcomes persist to a duel record (wins/losses/win rate shown on the Loadout page).
Item trading	Two nearby overworld players open an escrow window, each offer items + currency, both confirm, server atomically swaps ownership.
USDC Marketplace	Players list items for sale; buyers pay with USDC (currently mocked cents); a 5% fee routes to treasury. The system is structured for real Solana USDC with idempotency and race-safe escrow.
Economy
Gold — in-game currency earned from runs, spent on room upgrades.
$LAB — transaction token (entry fees, owner earnings, treasury). Never a drop.
USDC — marketplace currency; mocked now, structured for on-chain Solana.
Economy rules: owner 20% is a cut out of visitor loot (no minting); item rewards go 100% to visitors.
